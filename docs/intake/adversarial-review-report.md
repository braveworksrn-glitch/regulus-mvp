# Synthesis Report: Regulus Trial-Run Adversarial Review

**Consensus: 5/5 reviewers — QUESTIONNAIRE REQUIRED FIRST. No application can be submitted from the current flow.**

---

## 1. The Verdict, and Exactly Where the Information Base Runs Out

All five reviewers — grant writer, cold client, verification/program officer, funnel operator, and compliance — independently reached the same verdict: **questionnaire_required_first**. There is no dissent to adjudicate on the core question.

The break point is precise and all five located it in the same place:

| Stage | Sufficient? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Outreach email → Grant Gap Report | **Yes** | Runs on public 990 data; zero client burden |
| Fundability read | **Yes, with relabel** | Honest and useful, but built entirely on an unverified business plan — must be marked "plan-based, self-reported, unverified" |
| Opportunity sheet | **Yes** | Research-heavy, org-light; the 508(c)(1)(A) triage holds |
| Partner-ask letters | **The seam** | Sendable after two fixes: strike the overclaims, fill the signature blocks |
| **Walmart packet (drafting stage)** | **NO — this is where it breaks** | First deliverable that asserts facts *about the org itself*. Six load-bearing facts are open markers; the core need ("what the $5,000 buys") is an admitted inference; the narrative tense claims a track record no evidence supports |
| Any submission | **No** | Even the deliberately easiest application in the market dies at Deed/BMF verification without an EIN letter, organizing documents, and a bank account — none confirmed to exist |

The structural diagnosis (funnel operator, confirmed by all): the flow was bootstrapped from a business plan a cold prospect will not have, and **there is no information-collection step anywhere in the funnel between 990-based public data and post-signing drafting**. The six [CLIENT TO PROVIDE] markers are, in the grant writer's phrase, "a deferred intake interview wearing a checklist costume." The central epistemic flaw: a source map can trace every claim to the plan, but the plan is prospective — the flow cannot distinguish what the org *does* from what it *intends*.

## 2. Contradictions Between Reviewers — Resolved

**a) Are the partner letters usable as-is?**
Cold client: "90% done, I'd sign and send in one evening." Grant writer & compliance: they contain overclaims ("we can document every hour," "photographs of current cases") describing artifacts with no evidence of existing.
**The grant writer is right.** The client's enthusiasm is a conversion signal, not a quality check — he cannot see that the letters promise capabilities he may not have. If Frontier asks to see the case photos, the relationship opens with an overclaim. Letters move first, but only after the strike-and-replace fix.

**b) Where does the screen sit — before the Gap Report or before the fundability read?**
Compliance implies the screen should precede even the Gap Report. Funnel operator: the Gap Report is the flow's only genuinely zero-burden asset and must stay that way; the screen *becomes the Gap Report's CTA*, gating the fundability read.
**The funnel operator is right.** The Gap Report needs only public data and is the reply-bait. Screen answers gate the read — which is where org-specific claims first appear and where research hours are first spent. This also solves the funnel operator's scalability finding: the screen replaces the business plan as the read's raw material.

**c) Are the three owner decisions (fiscal sponsor, 1023-EZ, governance) form fields or a phone call?**
Verification officer: forced yes/no/defer fields. Cold client & compliance: a scheduled call with plain-English tradeoffs — "changing my board touches church polity; that's a month of prayer, not a checkbox."
**The cold client is right about the medium, the verification officer is right about the record.** Decisions are made on a scheduled 30-minute call with a one-paragraph tradeoff each, then captured as yes/no/decide-by-[date] fields with the deadline consequence attached (Berea dies ~Oct 10 without a sponsor). Never a bare web-form checkbox.

**d) Is the fundability read honest?**
Three reviewers praise its candor as the flow's best trust asset. Compliance flags "more fundable than most startups we read" as an undisclosed comparative a regulator could quote.
**Both are right; the fix is surgical, not structural.** Keep the adverse-facts-first voice untouched; soften only the comparative and the "funders notice that arithmetic" implied-likelihood phrases to criteria statements.

**e) How long to submittable?**
Grant writer: nothing can be finalized now. Cold client: earliest honest Walmart submission is ~one month out (bank account is the long pole), comfortably inside Nov 30.
**Not actually a contradiction — together they are the plan.** Nothing submits today; with intake run this week, Walmart submits in ~4 weeks. The Sep 8 HPG letters are the only item that moves first.

## 3. The Unified Two-Stage Intake

### Stage 1 — Pre-Call Screen
*10 questions, under 10 minutes, phone-friendly. Framed as reciprocity: "Correct our public-data read before we invest research hours in you." Every question is a router — it changes what the free read can honestly say or whether the prospect belongs in the funnel. Nothing here requires documents, trust, or judgment.*

| # | Question | Justification | Hard stop? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Are you applying as an **organization**, or seeking funds for yourself/your household? | UDAP guard — the opportunity sheet contains homeowner-direct federal programs; individuals must be routed out (referred directly to USDA/LIHEAP), never engaged | **HARD STOP** if individual |
| 2 | Exact **legal name**, and is the org incorporated/registered in your state? (yes / no / papers are somewhere) | Every verification portal (Deed/BMF) fails on name mismatch; separates "find documents" from "create entity" work | **HARD STOP** if no legal entity exists — route to formation track; no fundability read on an org that doesn't exist |
| 3 | Do you have an **EIN**? (yes + number / no / not sure) | Gates everything downstream; enables preparer-side BMF/Pub 78 lookup before any strategy is written | **HARD STOP** with #2 both no |
| 4 | **Tax status** picker: 501(c)(3) with letter / applied / church-508 / fiscally sponsored / none | The single fact that reshaped the entire HIFR opportunity sheet — it was lucky to be in the plan; ask it explicitly | No — routes strategy |
| 5 | **Bank account in the org's legal name?** (yes/no) | Hard gate for Walmart's 21-day electronic-payment window and most corporate portals | No — but flags a critical-path task |
| 6 | **Have you completed any projects yet?** Roughly how many, since when? | The single question that determines every narrative's verb tense — track-record vs. launch-stage framing. Explicit zero is a valid, recorded answer | No |
| 7 | **Counties/geography served** + contact block (name, phone, email, address) | Geography drove most of the opportunity sheet's value; letters are unsendable without a signature block | No |
| 8 | **Annual budget band** (4 ranges; "best guess is fine") | Sizes the realistic ask tier; confirms ICP | No |
| 9 | **Any federal or pass-through government funds?** (yes/no/unsure) | Mandatory 2 CFR 200.421 compliance screen per the v2 gauntlet — currently appears nowhere client-facing | No — routes to compliance review |
| 10 | **Are you the person who can sign for the org?** Who else must say yes (board/elders)? + checkbox: *"I understand this assessment describes eligibility and fit, not the likelihood of winning any grant."* | Kills wrong-contact drop-off; reveals governance reality before anyone proposes changing it; creates the contemporaneous no-odds record | No |

**Preparer-side, run in parallel (never asked of the client):** IRS BMF/Pub 78 lookup, state Secretary of State search, address verification. Free public lookups that turn "Deed may stall" into a known fact.

### Stage 2 — Post-Signing Deep Intake
*The existing data-room checklist upgraded into the intake instrument itself — one artifact, not two — delivered as a guided portal form plus one 45-minute recorded interview scheduled at signing. One section per evening; every item has a photo-of-paper upload and an "I don't have this" path that says what happens next. Grouped by what applications actually consume:*

**A. Identity & verification** (what Deed-style portals check): EIN letter upload (CP 575, or the 147C path with the 30–60-min IRS hold warned about), organizing documents, state registration, DBA, physical/mailing address, bank documentation, SAM.gov/UEI status.

**B. People** (asked on ~90% of applications): leadership bios via 3 guided prompts per person (role, relevant experience, why this work); board list with roles, compensation yes/no, and beneficiary-seat flag (gates SDOP); volunteer roster size and hours logged.

**C. Track record & evidence** (the raw material of every narrative): service-history table — per completed project: date, repair type, homeowner age/income band, photos, consent status; **zero recorded explicitly** so drafts are written honestly. Case-log starter for up to 5 homeowners with a consent script read aloud and initialed, structured to be completed from a truck with a phone camera. Materials inventory and pipeline commitments (signed vs. aspirational). Liability insurance status — asked by every repair-program funder and partner before any MOU.

**D. Money**: guided one-page budget builder, 6 lines max (materials, fuel, insurance, tools, misc, in-kind), "estimated" allowed; receipts and in-kind to date; prior funding and declines; use-of-funds as a 3-line item/quantity/cost materials budget — reviewers fund line items, not categories.

**E. Relationships**: partnership/affiliation inventory — "Do you know anyone at Gateway / Frontier / county fiscal court / a Thrivent member / other pastors who'd co-sign?" Cheap to answer; half the 90-day list runs through it.

**F. Attestation set** (one signable form, drafted now — see Fix #6): nondiscrimination/open-to-all, data-room accuracy, authorization to represent. Resolves three Walmart markers in one signature.

**G. The three owner decisions**: scheduled 30-minute call, plain-English tradeoff paragraph each (fiscal sponsor = they hold the money and take 5–10%; 1023-EZ = $275 and starts the clocks; governance = homeowners get majority vote), recorded as yes/no/decide-by-[date] with the deadline consequence stated.

**Stage 2.5 — Per-application micro-confirms** (client-gate, never front-loaded): application-specific [CLIENT TO CONFIRM] items — Walmart store selection, exact materials list — resolved as 2–3-question approvals when that application is chosen. Collected early, they go stale; collected in bulk, they're pure burden.

### Deliberately EXCLUDED — the completion-rate protections
- **Mission essays, vision questions, theory-of-change, logic models** — the business plan or interview gives voice; the drafter derives narrative
- **Anything already in the client's plan** — "do not make me retype my own mission"; every derivable field pre-filled as *confirm-or-correct*, never blank
- **Multi-year projections, strategic-plan uploads, policy manuals** — no target funder tier asks
- **Document uploads pre-signing** — existence yes/no only; upload burden pre-commitment kills conversion
- **Homeowner PII at screen stage**; demographic detail beyond age/income band — "I will talk for an hour about my neighbors' roofs but I will abandon a web form that asks their income"
- **Anything scrapable from 990/BMF/SoS/website** — Regulus looks it up
- **The 40-program landscape** — the deliverables carry the map; intake captures only what exists in the filing cabinet, the truck, and the client's head

## 4. Top 10 Fixes Before Outreach Starts — Ranked by Severity

1. **Rewrite both Walmart narratives to launch-stage honest tense** unless intake confirms completed projects: "is launching," "our first repairs this winter." Present-tense track-record claims with a possibly-empty case log are material misrepresentation the client attests to under his own name. Spark Good funds startups; it does not forgive misrepresented history. *(All 5 reviewers)*

2. **Fix the partner letters before Sep 8**: strike "we can document every hour" and "photographs of current cases" unless the artifacts exist; replace with what is verifiably true; fill both empty signature blocks and get the executive-director names. First impressions with Frontier and Gateway are worth more than polish — and these are the only deliverables that can move this week.

3. **Make "intake complete" a hard pipeline gate ahead of "packet drafted."** Convert the data-room checklist into the deep-intake instrument itself (form + uploads + signature + 45-min scheduled interview), and add the five universal fields it omits: founding date, leadership bios, served counts (explicit zero allowed), insurance status, prior funding history. The current flow inverts intake and drafting; the six markers prove it.

4. **Insert the pre-call screen into the funnel definition** (GRANT-PIPELINE-PLAN.md §3): the Gap Report CTA changes from "join the waitlist" to "answer 10 questions, get a fundability read." Simultaneously fix the Prospector ICP mismatch this trial exposed — church-status and pre-990 orgs like HIFR are invisible to the 990 signal; add a second sourcing channel (denominational directories, state registries) or exclude them from cold targeting.

5. **Verify the client's own status claims before building strategy on them**: preparer-side BMF/Pub 78/KY SoS lookups plus EIN-letter request at intake. The fundability read reasoned *from* 508(c)(1)(A) status without ever verifying it; if the org is not formally organized, the entire opportunity sheet re-ranks.

6. **Draft the one-page attestation form now** (nondiscrimination, data-room accuracy, authorization to represent). It is referenced in three deliverables and exists in none.

7. **Insert a signed engagement + scope/fee disclosure step between the free read and any packet.** Header the Walmart packet "no engagement signed — not for finalization" until executed. Currently the funnel jumps from free deliverable to submission-ready draft with nothing a regulator could reconstruct in between.

8. **Add the consumer-individual boundary**: entity-type router as screen question #1, plus an explicit disclaimer on the opportunity sheet's homeowner-direct cluster (USDA 504, VA HISA, LIHEAP) — Regulus advises the org; it does not prepare individual homeowners' federal applications. This is licensing/UDAP exposure, not style.

9. **Scrub outcome-drift language and lock the no-odds record**: soften "more fundable than most startups we read" to a criteria statement; "genuinely equals one ramp" to "can fund"; never render fit scores as probabilities client-facing; store the no-odds acknowledgment with the engagement record; template CAN-SPAM elements (physical address, opt-out, "assessment offer, not a grant offer") into outreach emails now.

10. **Add the client-usability layer**: a one-page "This Week" cover sheet (3 actions, 3 phone numbers, 1 deadline) on top of the packet; a one-line jargon gloss (UEI, BMF, 147C, MOU, CRA, fiscal sponsor); fill-in templates for the scary items (the "no financial statements exist" signed statement, consent script, budget). Every uncushioned term is a place a less stubborn founder closes the laptop.

## 5. What Is Genuinely Strong — Do Not Touch

- **The marker discipline and claim-by-claim source map** (Walmart packet Part 4, "cannot finalize while markers remain"). Every reviewer independently called this rare and better than most professional shops. It becomes a hard system rule, not a casualty of the intake redesign.
- **Verification-first sequencing**: Deed/BMF risk placed first, the 21-day banking window, email-match mechanics. Most preparers discover this after drafting; this flow leads with it.
- **The honest-negative voice**: "No determination letter... no drafting skill removes it." The cold client said this one line bought more trust than anything else, and the funnel operator identified it as the conversion asset a generic tool cannot fake. It is simultaneously the compliance defense and the sales engine. Fix the two drift phrases (Fix #9); change nothing else about the register.
- **The strategy triage**: 508(c)(1)(A) closes a third of the market → partnerships and homeowner-direct first, foundations via sponsor. More sophisticated than most professional shops produce for small clients; the dropped-with-reasons section and MDS self-correction show real verification.
- **The compliance rails threaded through every artifact**: client submits himself, no outcome promises, human sign-off recorded. Scaling the flow does not scale legal exposure — keep this invariant.
- **Character-counted narratives fitted to actual form fields**, and the community-at-large framing correctly flagged as load-bearing for the faith-based track.
- **The case-log-starts-the-clock insight** (HUD 3-year history, SDOP governance gate) — the long-game strategic call that makes the deep intake's case-log starter urgent rather than optional.
- **The data-room checklist's substance** — its sections and separation of documents / evidence / registrations / client-owned decisions are the correct skeleton. It changes *form* (checklist → intake instrument, Fix #3), not content.

**Bottom line**: the flow produces excellent research and honest drafts-with-holes, not submittable applications. The fix is not more drafting skill — it is a 10-question screen before the free read and a structured intake as a hard gate before drafting. With intake run this week: partner letters (post-fix) move by Sep 8, and the earliest honest Walmart submission lands ~4 weeks out, comfortably inside Nov 30.
