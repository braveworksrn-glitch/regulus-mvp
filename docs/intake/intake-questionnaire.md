# Regulus Intake Instrument — v1 (outreach-ready)

*Adopted from the 5/5 adversarial-panel verdict (docs/intake/adversarial-review-report.md): no application may be drafted to final, and no fundability read issued, until the relevant stage below is complete. "Intake complete" is a hard pipeline gate ahead of "packet drafted."*

**Funnel placement:** Grant Gap Report stays zero-burden (public 990 data only). Its call-to-action becomes: **"Answer 10 questions, get your free fundability read."** The screen gates the read; the deep intake gates drafting; per-application micro-confirms gate each submission.

---

## Stage 1 — Pre-Call Screen (10 questions, <10 minutes, phone-friendly)

*Framing shown to the prospect: "Our free read starts from your public filings. These ten questions correct anything the public data gets wrong before we invest research hours in your organization." Pre-fill anything derivable from public data as confirm-or-correct — never make them retype what we can look up.*

1. **Are you applying as an organization, or seeking funds for yourself or your household?**
   → *Individual = automatic, courteous decline with referrals (USDA 504, LIHEAP, benefits finder). No fee, no read, ever.* **[HARD STOP]**
2. **Exact legal name of the organization — and is it incorporated or registered with your state?** (yes / no / "the papers are somewhere")
   → *No legal entity = formation track, not a fundability read.* **[HARD STOP if #2 and #3 are both no]**
3. **Do you have an EIN?** (yes + the number / no / not sure)
4. **Which best describes your tax status?** 501(c)(3) with IRS letter / applied, waiting / church or ministry (508(c)(1)(A)) / fiscally sponsored / none of these
5. **Does the organization have a bank account in its own legal name?** (yes / no)
6. **Have you completed any projects or served anyone yet?** Roughly how many, starting when? *(Zero is a fine answer — it changes how we write, not whether we help.)*
7. **What counties or communities do you serve?** + name, phone, email, mailing address.
8. **Annual budget, best guess:** under $25k / $25–100k / $100–500k / over $500k
9. **Does any of your current money come from federal or government pass-through sources?** (yes / no / not sure)
10. **Are you the person who can sign for the organization? Who else has to say yes** (board, elders, members)?
    ☐ *"I understand this assessment describes eligibility and fit — not the likelihood of winning any grant."* (required checkbox; stored with the record)

**Run in parallel, preparer-side (never asked of the client):** IRS BMF / Pub 78 lookup on the EIN, Secretary of State registry search, address check. Turns "verification may stall" into a known fact before the read is written.

**Routing:** individuals → decline lane · no-entity → formation guidance · nonprofits/churches → nonprofit track read · for-profits → SBIR-track screen · federal-funds "yes/unsure" → compliance note attached to the file.

## Stage 2 — Deep Intake (post-signing; guided form + one 45-minute recorded call)

*The data-room checklist upgraded into the instrument itself. One section per sitting; every document item has a photo-of-the-paper upload and an "I don't have this" path that says what happens next. Interview scheduled at signing.*

**A. Identity & verification** — EIN letter (CP 575; or the 147C phone route — warn about the hold time), organizing documents, state registration, DBA if any, physical/mailing address, bank documentation, SAM.gov/UEI status (start if absent).

**B. People** — leadership bios via three prompts each (role · relevant experience · why this work); board list with roles, compensated y/n, and whether beneficiaries hold seats; volunteer roster size and hours logged so far.

**C. Track record & evidence** — service-history table (per completed project: date, type, homeowner age band, income band, photos, consent status; **explicit zero recorded if zero** so every narrative is written honestly as launch-stage); case-log starter for up to 5 upcoming homeowners with a read-aloud consent script; materials/tools inventory; pipeline commitments marked *signed* vs *hoped-for*; liability insurance status.

**D. Money** — one-page budget builder (6 lines max: materials, fuel, insurance, tools, misc, in-kind; "estimated" allowed); receipts and in-kind to date with dollar-equivalents; prior funding and prior applications/declines; per-project use-of-funds as item/quantity/cost.

**E. Relationships** — who do you already know at: community action agency / regional housing org / county fiscal court / churches with fraternal-benefit members / anyone who would write a support letter.

**F. Attestation set (one signature)** — services open to all regardless of protected characteristics · data-room contents are accurate · signer is authorized to represent the organization. *(Form template: docs/intake/attestation-form.md.)*

**G. Owner decisions (30-minute scheduled call, then recorded as yes / no / decide-by-date with the deadline consequence attached)** — fiscal sponsorship (sponsor holds funds, typical 5–10% fee; unlocks foundation deadlines) · early 1023-EZ filing for the planned entity ($275; starts multi-year eligibility clocks) · any governance change a program requires (board/beneficiary questions touch church polity — a conversation, never a checkbox).

## Stage 2.5 — Per-application micro-confirms (client gate, per submission)

2–3 questions resolved when a specific application is chosen, never front-loaded (they go stale): e.g., for Walmart — which store your families shop at · the exact materials list the ask funds · final read-and-submit.

## Deliberately excluded — protect the completion rate

Mission essays and vision statements (their own documents give voice) · anything derivable from 990/BMF/SoS/website (we look it up; they confirm) · multi-year projections, strategic plans, theory-of-change · document uploads before signing (existence yes/no only) · homeowner PII at screen stage · demographic detail beyond age/income band.
