# Opportunity Sheet — Home Is the Foundation Restoration

**Prepared 2026-08-20 by the Regulus pipeline (trial run).** West Liberty, Morgan County, Kentucky — ARC-distressed county, USDA-rural, no current FEMA Individual Assistance designation.
**Method:** 6 researchers by funder type → 57 programs found → 38 unique after dedup → eligibility screen against the org's actual status → top 8 independently re-verified against funders' own pages. Program facts carry sources; deadlines re-verify before any application. Nothing here is a prediction of an award.

**The one structural fact that shapes this whole list:** operating under 508(c)(1)(A) means no IRS determination letter. That closes roughly a third of the researched market — the private-foundation and corporate cash-grant third. It does **not** close homeowner-direct federal/state programs, operating partnerships, or fraternal micro-grants — which is why the 90-day list below leans on those. Cures at the end.

## The 508(c)(1)(A) reality check and its cures

What the missing letter costs: essentially all private foundations, IRS-verified corporate portals, the strongest affiliations, and HUD. What it doesn't: everything in the 90-day list above. Cheapest cures, in order:
1. **Fiscal sponsorship** (immediate; typically 5–10% admin fee; Frontier Housing is the natural candidate) — unlocks Berea (Oct 10), Steele-Reese (Mar 2027), and ACF TA grants now.
2. **The already-planned 501(c)(3) CDC, filed early.** Form 1023-EZ ($275, weeks) — available because the CDC is a non-church entity; churches themselves cannot use 1023-EZ. Filing early also starts the multi-year clocks (HUD's 3-year service history, Good360 tenure, funder age tests), and "applied" status alone already satisfies CCHD.
