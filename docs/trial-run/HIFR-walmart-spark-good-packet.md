## Part 1 — Verification prep (do this first; it's the real gate)

Deed (Walmart's verifier) checks IRS records, address, and banking before any application can be submitted. A church-status org that never filed for recognition is often **absent from the IRS Business Master File**, which commonly stalls automated verification. Prepare this file before creating the account:

1. IRS **EIN assignment letter** (CP 575, or request a 147C replacement by phone) — `[CLIENT TO PROVIDE]`
2. Organizing documents: articles/constitution/bylaws or church charter — `[CLIENT TO PROVIDE]`
3. Financials in lieu of a Form 990 (churches are 990-exempt): the current-year budget and a signed statement of receipts to date — from the data-room checklist
4. **Bank account documentation in the organization's legal name** — payment is electronic-only, and banking setup must be completed within **21 days** of an approval email or the award is withdrawn — `[CLIENT TO PROVIDE]`
5. If verification stalls on the BMF check: contact Deed support directly (their suggested route), and hold the fallback — applying through a Deed-verified partner church — in reserve.
6. Account mechanics: the Walmart.com Spark Good account email must **match** the Deed account email; only the verified primary owner can apply.

**Expanded version (if a longer field appears):**
> Many Morgan County families live in aging homes with active roof leaks, deteriorating floors, and no affordable local repair options. Left alone, these conditions push elderly residents on fixed incomes out of homes they own outright. Home Is the Foundation Restoration exists for exactly these cases. We focus on "dry-in" repairs that keep a home livable — stopping leaks, stabilizing floors and structure, building accessibility ramps, and winterizing to cut utility burdens — provided at no cost to the homeowner. Our model stretches every dollar: materials are reclaimed through barn deconstruction and donated surplus, labor comes from volunteer crews and the homeowners themselves, and local youth learn trade skills alongside experienced volunteers on every project. A Spark Good grant funds the materials that cannot be salvaged — fasteners, sealants, flashing, treated lumber, insulation — turning $5,000 into completed repairs for multiple households in the [STORE] service area. The community at large benefits: projects serve any qualifying Morgan County household, and our volunteer teams draw from churches and neighbors across the county.

**Two compliance notes for the writer:**
- The community-at-large framing is load-bearing: Walmart's faith-based track requires projects benefiting the broader community, not the congregation. Both drafts state service to "any qualifying Morgan County household" — keep that.
- Walmart's exclusions bar organizations that deny service based on protected characteristics. Client must confirm services are open to all — `[CLIENT TO CONFIRM in writing at attestation]`.
