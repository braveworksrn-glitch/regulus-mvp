## Individual Reactions

**Dale (flipper):** "First one of these I've seen that admits the county price. That's suicide for margin and I respect it — but your buyer will still open the county site, see you paid $3,200, and choke on $6,000 even itemized. The real product isn't the lot, it's the cured title. You need to sell the $2,800 spread as 'what it costs to make a tax lot actually ownable' — because it does. Improvement menu is the first honest one I've ever seen: survey, perc, clearing, driveway are the only four things that reliably add value to raw dirt. Someone in that room has actually done this."

**Marisol (title attorney):** "Structurally, this is the cleanest version of the idea I've reviewed. Series LLC with member-as-manager and no fractions kills most of my Howey concerns before the opinion letter. Two flags: (1) quiet title in Florida runs 6–12 months and $2,500–4,500 *per lot* — 25 lots is $75k+ of legal before revenue; the Michigan land-bank route isn't a preference, it's the only viable v1. (2) The $500 deed-out is the whole credibility of the product — put it in the operating agreement as an irrevocable right, not a fee schedule you can change."

**Chuck (educator):** "My audience — the 'buy land for $5k and own something real' crowd — is exactly who this is for, and the sandbox demo will convert them better than any $99 lot would have. But 6–8 week latency on the dopamine loop is brutal. My students quit courses with faster feedback than that. The weekly-tick honesty is admirable and will kill retention. You need *something* real that moves weekly: county doc status, crew queue position, drone flyover of the district even when it's not your lot."

**Priya (VC):** "This is the first land-gamification deck where I can't find the security. They amputated everything I'd flag. My problem is the inverse: they also amputated the business. Curation fee + 20% on maybe $8k of lifetime improvements per lot, 50 lots, one county — that's a ~$300k revenue business over 18 months with $1.5–3k CAC. The month-12 kill gate is the most honest slide, and it's also the reason I pass. This is a lifestyle business or a very patient seed bet, not a venture case. As a *product*, though — I'd send it to every founder who pitches me fractional land."

**Ray (surveyor/buyer):** "Retainer crews before first sale — whoever wrote that has been stood up by a dozer guy. The board states mapping to real verified states is legit; a surveyed, perked, cleared lot with a driveway culvert genuinely is a different asset than raw scrub, and 'Build-Ready' is the correct end state. My worry is the 'contiguous cluster' — 25–50 lots that cheap in one county means one distressed area, and the honest raw comps will show your buyers exactly that. The comps transparency cuts both ways."

**Janet (land bank):** "As a land bank board member: we would *love* this buyer. Land banks are desperate for end-users who improve rather than speculate, and clean title in 60–90 days is real. But land bank lots are urban/small-town infill — 0.15-acre demolished-house lots, not homestead acreage. The board fantasy ('Tame the Wild') and the Michigan land-bank inventory don't match. Pick one: Florida rural fantasy with a 12-month title slog, or Michigan speed with lots nobody dreams about. That contradiction is the biggest unresolved decision hiding in a 'DECIDED' section."

**Tom (target buyer):** "I bought 2 acres off a YouTube guy in 2021, still can't get a driveway permit. If someone had sold me a lot with title insurance, a survey, a perc test, and a real crew I could dispatch from my phone — I'd have paid double. $5k plus maybe $6k of improvements to a build-ready lot I can walk on? Yes. What I'd need first: let me talk to a human, show me the actual county, and prove the $500 deed-out works — show me one person who did it."

**Vince (lender):** "All three land-as-a-service startups I financed died the same way: inventory carry outran service revenue. Their model admits 12 months of carry, which puts them ahead of the corpses, but 'inventory funded separately and capped' is doing enormous load-bearing work in one sentence. Who funds it, at what cost, and what happens to owners' servicing and prepaid taxes if HoldCo dies? An orphan-plan (successor servicer, escrowed tax reserve, auto deed-out on insolvency) isn't a nice-to-have — it's what I'd underwrite."

## Top Objections & Trust Barriers

1. **Latency vs. retention** — 6–8 week honest cadence may kill the game loop the whole thesis depends on (Chuck, Priya).
2. **FL-vs-MI contradiction** — homestead fantasy inventory and land-bank clean-title inventory are different assets; "one county TBD" is the biggest undecided decision labeled DECIDED (Janet, Marisol).
3. **Unit economics** — legal cure cost + carry + CAC vs. ~$3k lifetime revenue per lot; venture-shaped costs on a lifestyle-shaped business (Priya, Vince).
4. **Orphan risk** — what happens to deeds, prepaid taxes, and servicing if the company dies (Vince, Tom).
5. **Transparent comps backfire** — honest raw comps will reveal buyers overpaid vs. raw dirt, even if fairly (Ray, Dale).
6. **Deed-out credibility** — the $500 exit must be contractually irrevocable and publicly demonstrated (Marisol, Tom).

## What Would Convert Skeptics

- **One completed public case study**: a lot bought, cured, surveyed, perked, cleared, driveway-in, then deeded out to a real owner's name — full timeline and costs published.
- Michigan land-bank pilot with a named county partner (institutional legitimacy).
- Insolvency/orphan plan in the operating agreement: escrowed tax reserve + successor servicer + automatic deed-out trigger.
- A weekly-moving real signal (doc status, crew queue, district drone flights) to bridge latency.
- Reframe pricing around the *journey to build-ready* (~$12–15k) rather than the lot markup.

## Predicted Sentiment

- **Positive: 45%** (Tom, Janet, Chuck; half of Marisol) — target buyers and institutional partners genuinely want this.
- **Neutral: 35%** (Marisol, Dale, Ray) — respect the honesty, doubt the margin/inventory fit.
- **Negative: 20%** (Priya, Vince) — not against the product; against the business model's economics.

Notably, this is the *reverse* of typical land-scheme panels: the practitioners' usual "this is a securities lawsuit in a costume" reaction is absent. The skepticism has migrated entirely from legality/ethics to viability.

## Top 5 Changes to Improve Reception

1. **Resolve the geography contradiction now**: commit to the Michigan land-bank pipeline for v1 speed and title cleanliness, and redesign the fantasy layer around infill renewal ("bring a block back") — or accept FL and budget 12 months + $4k/lot of quiet title honestly in the model.
2. **Ship a public "Lot Zero" proof-of-life before selling anything**: one founder-owned lot taken through the full loop including the $500 deed-out, published as the flagship content asset (also solves CAC).
3. **Add an owner-protection/orphan plan** to the operating agreement — escrowed prepaid taxes, successor servicer, insolvency-triggered auto deed-out — and market it; it converts the lender/attorney skeptics and is Tom's #1 trust ask.
4. **Bridge the latency gap with honest weekly signals**: county doc tracking, crew queue position, monthly district drone flyovers visible to all owners — real information at game cadence, no fake progress.
5. **Reprice the narrative around the build-ready journey** (~$12–15k all-in to a surveyed, perked, cleared, accessed lot) instead of defending the lot markup — and pair transparent county-price disclosure with a plain "what title cure actually costs" breakdown so the spread reads as value, not margin.
