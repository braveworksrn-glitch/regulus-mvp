# Regulus v1 — Virtual Retail Panel Session
**8 participants: crypto-curious gamers, Monopoly GO players, side-hustle investors ($500–$5,000)**

---

## Individual Reactions

### 1. Tyler, 24 — Crypto-curious gamer (Axie/Runescape veteran, $800 budget)
> "Wait, so there's no way to make money? No token, no marketplace, no flipping? The board looks sick, the before/after drone photos would go crazy on TikTok... but you're asking me to pay $4k for something I literally can't sell in-game. That's a $4k cosmetic. I've bought skins, but not FOUR THOUSAND DOLLAR skins."

- **Would pay:** $0 at $4–6k floor. Priced out entirely. Would spend $50–100/mo if there were a cheaper on-ramp.
- **Objection:** No exit liquidity, price 5x his budget.
- **Trust barrier:** None — actually trusts it MORE because there's no token ("no rug possible"). Just can't afford it.

### 2. Denise, 41 — Monopoly GO daily player, nurse, $2,000 budget
> "I love the idea of a real Boardwalk. The clearing-the-brush before/after? I'd watch that all day. But $4,000 minimum? I spend maybe $30 a month on Monopoly GO. And 'improvements may cost more than they add to value' — honey, that sentence would stop me cold. Why would I pay for something that loses money?"

- **Would pay:** $1,500–2,500 max for a whole lot. At $4–6k, she's a spectator.
- **Objection:** Killing the $99 starter lot killed her entry point. The honest disclosure paradoxically scares her.
- **Converts if:** Payment plans (out of scope in v1 — financing is excluded) or a sub-$2,500 lot tier.

### 3. Marcus, 33 — Side-hustle investor ($5,000, does turo + dividend stocks)
> "Okay, I actually respect this. Itemized pricing, title insurance bound before listing, $500 deed-out — that's the most honest real-estate product I've ever seen pitched. But here's my problem: you've stripped out every reason for ME to buy. No appreciation framing, no resale path, no yield. I'd buy land as an investment; you're explicitly telling me not to. So... who is this for?"

- **Would pay:** $4,500 for ONE lot — but only because he privately believes cured-title land in a growth county appreciates anyway. He's buying the investment thesis the marketing refuses to make.
- **Objection:** "The honest version of this product is just... buying land, which I can do on LandWatch cheaper."
- **Trust barrier:** Wants to verify the curation fee vs. county records — will actually do it.

### 4. Jade, 27 — Crypto-curious, got burned on NFTs, $1,200 budget
> "The fact that they went out of their way to NOT be an NFT is the whole pitch for me. Real deed, real LLC, real photos, escrow, exit anytime for $500. That's everything the metaverse land scams pretended to be. But I lost $3k on Otherdeeds, so my land budget is $1,200 and my trust budget is zero. I'd do the free sandbox lot and lurk for six months watching other people's timelines."

- **Would pay:** $0 now; $3,000+ in year two if she watches 20 real transformations happen on schedule.
- **Trust barrier:** Needs longitudinal proof — "show me someone who deed-ed out and it actually worked."
- **Converts if:** Public, verifiable deed-out case studies; county recorder links on every lot page.

### 5. Rob, 52 — Monopoly GO + retired-early spouse, $5,000 budget
> "I've always wanted a little piece of land 'up north' and never trusted those tax-deed guys on YouTube. Title cured, insurance bound, taxes prepaid two years, someone else deals with the county — and I get a fun map instead of a folder of PDFs? Sold. I don't care about resale. I care that when I drive up there, the driveway they charged me for exists."

- **Would pay:** $5,000–6,500, would stretch. **Strongest convert on the panel.**
- **Objection:** Wants to visit before buying — "can I walk the lot first?"
- **Trust barrier:** Vendor quality. "Who are these crews? Show me their license numbers."

### 6. Priya, 29 — Side-hustle investor ($3,000, house-hacks, reads BiggerPockets)
> "The unit economics for ME are bad and they admit it — 20–25% platform fee on improvements, curation fee on top of county price, $6–8/month forever. I can buy rural Michigan land at auction myself for $1,200. What I'm actually paying for is the title cure and the dopamine. The title cure is worth maybe $1,500. The dopamine is not worth $2,000."

- **Would pay:** $3,000 ceiling, and only for a lot where itemization shows curation fee ≤$1,000.
- **Objection:** Stack of fees; sees herself as the DIY alternative.
- **Converts if:** The curation fee is demonstrably cheaper than DIY quiet title (~$2–4k in legal fees) — "actually, say THAT louder."

### 7. Kevin, 21 — Monopoly GO whale ($200/mo in-app), student, $500 budget
> "Bro, the tile states, the district co-op quests where neighbors split a bulldozer — that's genuinely the coolest game mechanic I've heard in years. But there's no way in. No $99 lot, no fractions, no rentals. The sandbox demo will be fun for 90 seconds and then I'll close the tab because the real game starts at $4,000. You built a whale game and deleted the minnow ladder."

- **Would pay:** $0. Total mismatch.
- **Objection:** He IS the dark-pattern-susceptible user the design deliberately excludes — and he knows it. "You fired me as a customer. Respect, I guess?"

### 8. Angela, 38 — Side-hustle investor ($4,000, Etsy shop, homestead-YouTube subscriber)
> "I watch clearing-land videos to fall asleep. Owning the plot IN the video, directing the crew, watching my overgrown tile turn green — that's not a game to me, that's a dream with a progress bar. The co-op driveway split with neighbors is brilliant. My only fear: what happens to my LLC and my map if Regulus dies in year two?"

- **Would pay:** $4,000–5,000. **Second-strongest convert.**
- **Objection:** Platform mortality risk — "the deed survives, but does the magic?"
- **Converts if:** A written wind-down plan: auto-deed-out at cost if the company folds, photo archive exportable.

## Common Objections (ranked)

1. **Price floor excludes the gamified audience** — killing the $99 lot removed the entire minnow funnel; the game mechanics now market to people who can't buy.
2. **"Why not just buy land myself?"** — DIY comparison from every side-hustler; curation fee value not quantified against DIY quiet-title cost.
3. **No liquidity story at all** — even non-speculators want to know exit works; $500 deed-out helps but off-platform resale feels theoretical.
4. **Improvement fee stack (20–25%)** feels like the real business model hiding behind the honest lot pricing.
5. **Platform mortality** — what happens to the board, photos, and servicing if Regulus folds.

## Trust Barriers
- Unproven crews/vendors (want license numbers, reviews)
- Need to see real deed-outs completed, not promised
- Want independent verification links (county recorder, parcel ID) on every lot page — *the design supports this; surface it harder*
- The honest "improvements may lose money" disclosure reads as a red flag to less sophisticated buyers rather than as candor

## What Converts Skeptics
- Public case studies: real owner → real deed-out → walked the land
- Fee-vs-DIY comparison table ("quiet title alone costs $2–4k in legal fees — here's ours itemized")
- Site-visit / open-house days at the county cluster
- Written wind-down covenant (auto deed-out at cost)
- A sub-$2,500 lot tier or (post-counsel) payment plans

## Top 5 Changes to Improve Reception

1. **Build a sub-$2,500 entry tier** (smaller/interior lots, thinner curation scope) — the single biggest conversion lever; 4 of 8 panelists are blocked purely on price, and the recruited audience's median budget is ~$2,000.
2. **Quantify the curation fee against DIY** — publish a "do it yourself vs. Regulus" cost sheet (auction risk, quiet-title legal fees, title insurance hassle). Turns the fee from markup into obvious value; converts Priya and Marcus types.
3. **Manufacture liquidity proof, not liquidity** — within 90 days of launch, publicly document 2–3 completed deed-outs and one off-platform resale at a real price, with county recorder links. This is Jade's (and every burned-crypto buyer's) entire conversion condition.
4. **Publish a platform wind-down covenant** — contractual auto-deed-out at cost + exportable photo/doc archive if Regulus ceases operations. Cheap to offer, removes the #1 fear of the strongest converts (Angela).
5. **Reframe the honesty disclosures for retail comprehension** — pair "improvements may cost more than they add to resale value" with "…and here's why owners do them anyway: access, build-readiness, and use." Also lead marketing with the land-dream/homestead psychographic (Rob/Angela) rather than gamers — the panel shows the game skin retains buyers but does not acquire them; the dream of owning and taming land does.
