# Regulus v1 — Unified Consensus Concept

**"HomesteadOS with a board-game soul": a title-cured land shop where real lots transform through verified real-world work, rendered as a living board — with the investment layer amputated.**

The war room converged: the magic survives (real lots, real crews, real photos, a board UI); the score changes from *what it's worth* to *what you built*. Every Tier-1 kill (securities-by-UI, value-destroying upsells, closed-loop bubble) is resolved by structural deletion, not disclosure.

---

## 1. Legal Structure (DECIDED — the legal memo is the constitution)

- **Regulus HoldCo (DE)** + **RegulusOps LLC** (services) + **one Series LLC**, one series per lot.
- Buyer purchases **100% of a series membership interest** — whole lots only, no fractions, ever in v1.
- Operating agreement makes the **member the manager**; every improvement is an à-la-carte service order the buyer initiates and approves. RegulusOps acts as buyer's agent contracting licensed local vendors — never as GC.
- **No lot is listed until title is cured** (quiet title or certification service) **and title insurance is bound**. The digital "Deed" mints only at that moment. No "as-is tax title" sales.
- **Always-available exit**: deed-out to the owner's own name for a flat $500 + recording costs. This right is what makes the Deed credible.
- **Howey opinion letter on the final structure before the first sale.** Marketing speaks only of ownership, building, land, and play — never returns, appreciation, flipping, or investment. ILSA + state land-sale analysis completed in writing; ≤100 lots total until it clears; marketing restricted to cleared states.
- The word "NFT" never appears. No blockchain. The Deed is a database record mirroring the LLC ledger.

## 2. Currency (DECIDED: fiat, full stop)

- **USD for everything real**, via a licensed payment processor; lot-sale and improvement funds flow through a licensed title/escrow partner — Regulus never holds buyer funds.
- **No token. No tradable or transferable credits. No bonus-loaded prepaid balances.**
- Only game currency: **XP/badges** — non-monetary, non-transferable, cosmetic + access-gating (early access to new lot releases, priority crew scheduling). Nothing redeemable for money or discounts on real assets in v1.

## 3. Pricing (DECIDED: radical transparency, service margin not information asymmetry)

- Lot price = **itemized**: county acquisition price (shown — buyers can look it up anyway) + title cure + entity + insurance + curation fee. Flat curation fee, not a 2x markup. Honest floor is ~$4–6k; **the $99 starter lot is dead** — replaced by a free 90-second sandbox demo lot so the loop is felt before purchase.
- Improvement pricing: vendor cost + **disclosed platform fee (~20–25%)**, published per county, with plain-English notice: *"Improvements may cost more than they add to resale value."*
- **NO value ticker. NO "est. value" deltas on action cards. NO appreciation events. NO buyback floor or guarantee.** Comps shown only as raw public records (actual nearby sales, unsynthesized).
- Revenue = curation fee, improvement service fees, at-cost-plus-thin servicing (~$6–8/lot/mo covering tax billing, insurance passthrough, monitoring — with **24 months of taxes+servicing prepaid in the purchase price**), later financing with counsel.

## 4. v1 Improvement Menu (DECIDED: gamify the paper stack — only ROI-positive actions)

| Action | Board effect |
|---|---|
| Boundary survey + corner pins | "Borders Revealed" — tile gets crisp surveyed edges |
| Perc / soil test | "Septic Approved" badge — tile earns a build-ready seal |
| Brush clearing (cosmetic) | The hero visual: overgrown tile → cleared green tile, before/after scrubber |
| Culvert + gravel driveway (permit pre-validated per county) | "Access Unlocked" — road connects to your tile |
| Title/tax milestones | Progress bars, county-doc trophies |

- Milestone-verified only: geotagged 4-corner before/after photos + county docs update the board. **No synced crane animations, no progress bars in hours** — weekly ticks, ETAs quoted in week ranges, auto-refund on blowout.
- **OUT of the menu**: tiny houses, sheds, fences, wells, septic installs, anything vertical. (Tiny houses return post-v1 only on independently appraised, zoning-cleared, build-ready lots — a separate premium track.)
- Job caps at contracted crew capacity; max 50% vendor deposits, balance released by escrow partner on photo verification.

## 5. Geography (DECIDED: one county)

- **One county in one deed state — Florida tax deeds or a Michigan land-bank pipeline** (land banks preferred: cleaner title, compressed cure cycle). Chosen for: no post-sale redemption, online auctions, functioning quiet-title path, light zoning, and a county where two crews (brush/dozer + surveyor) are **retainer-contracted before the first lot sells**.
- 25–50 contiguous-clustered lots, founder-operated, for 12–18 months. Districts emerge naturally from the cluster — the "living regional board" is one real place, fully alive, not a sparse continent.

## 6. Marketplace (DECIDED: none at launch)

- **v1 has no resale marketplace, no percentage success fee, no suggested price bands, no buyback.**
- Exits available day one: (a) deed-out to your own name ($500 flat), (b) sell off-platform however you like — Regulus will process the series-interest assignment for a **flat documentation fee** when both parties bring their own deal (classifieds-style, no matchmaking, no pricing guidance).
- A true in-platform marketplace is a post-v1 decision gated on: ≥150 cured lots, broker-dealer/RE-brokerage analysis in writing, and demonstrated off-platform resale at real prices.

## 7. The Game That Remains (the magic, kept)

- **Living board**: stylized low-poly isometric county map over real parcel data; tile states (Overgrown / Cleared / Surveyed / Access Unlocked / Build-Ready) are literal, verified real-world states.
- **The before/after scrubber** — drone + 4-corner photo timeline — is the shareable core and the state oracle.
- **Score = Development, not dollars**: XP from completed verified actions; ranks (Thimble → Top Hat) gate early access to new lot releases and priority scheduling — never fees on transactions.
- **Quests**: onboarding chain ("Stake Your Claim" → "First Boots on Ground" → "Tame the Wild"), each tied to a real completed action. District quests are **cooperative cost-sharing** (neighbors split one crew mobilization — real assemblage economics), not platform-funded appreciation.
- **Timeline-as-story**: every county doc, crew dispatch, and photo in one feed — audit trail and narrative in one.
- **Honest events deck**: real notifications (photos uploaded, permit granted, tax bill paid) at real cadence. All "slot-machine / dark-pattern" vocabulary and mechanics (streak drips, drop events, scarcity timers) are deleted from design docs and product; cooling-off period and per-account caps (3 lots / first 90 days) included.

## 8. Explicitly OUT of v1

1. Value ticker, est.-value projections, appreciation events, "flip" framing anywhere
2. Buyback guarantees of any kind (7-day or floor)
3. Resale marketplace, % success fees, comps synthesis
4. Tiny houses and all vertical improvements
5. Tokens, tradable credits, prepaid bonuses, blockchain
6. Fractional ownership, pooled anything, rental/yield products
7. Multi-state expansion, >100 lots, sub-$1k "impulse" lots
8. Financing products (until counsel + scale)

## 9. Capitalization & North Star (honest framing)

- Modeled as a **capital-intensive land-ops business with software leverage**: ~12 months of inventory carry before first revenue, refund/rescission reserves, one field manager in-model, inventory funded separately from opco equity and capped at what that capital can eat.
- CAC underwritten at **$1,500–3,000**, acquisition content-led (land/homestead/tiny-home YouTube niche — every verified transformation is organic content).
- **North star: completed verified real-world actions per owner per quarter.** Kill/pivot gate at month 12: if gamified buyers don't retain through 6–8 week real-world latency, drop the game skin and run the honest land-improvement service underneath.

**One sentence:** Regulus v1 sells whole, title-cured lots in one county at transparent itemized prices, lets owners direct real, ROI-positive improvements executed by retainer-contracted crews and verified by geotagged photos that transform a delightful board UI — with no ticker, no token, no marketplace, and no promises except that the land, the deed, and the work are real.
